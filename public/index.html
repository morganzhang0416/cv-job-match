How is the score worked out?

The tool reads the job description and finds the skills and keywords it asks for. Then it checks which of them are in your resume.

  • Must-have skills count the most. Nice-to-haves count the least.
  • A keyword that the job repeats counts a little more.
  • Soft skills and loose phrases count less than named skills and tools.
  • A keyword also counts when your resume names a skill that proves it. For example, React proves JavaScript.

The score compares words. It does not judge how good your experience is. Treat it as a guide, not a verdict.

Your resume is read on the computer that runs this app. The app does not save it. If you turn on AI, only the job description and a few bullet points go to the AI service.
